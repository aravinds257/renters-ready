GUIDANCE NOTES FOR TENANTS (Renters' Rights Act 2025/2026):
  1. If you accept the proposed new rent, you do not need to do anything. The new rent will take effect on the starting date shown.
  2. If you do not accept the proposed new rent, you may refer this notice to the First-tier Tribunal (Property Chamber) before the proposed starting date using Form Rents1.
  3. The Tribunal will determine the market rent for the property. The rent determined by the Tribunal can be lower than, equal to, or higher than the landlord's proposal.
