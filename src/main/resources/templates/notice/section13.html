Section 13 Rent Review Notice

Official Form 4 statutory notice under Section 13(2) of the Housing Act 1988 / Renters' Rights Act.

Renters' Rights Act Compliance Rules:
  • At least 2 full months' advance notice is legally required for monthly tenancies.
  • Rent cannot be increased more than once in any 12-month period.
Must be at least 2 months from notice date.
